I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Sciences at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Prof. Suman Banerjee.

My research focuses on machine learning systems (MLSys) for multimodal large language models (MLLMs) and video understanding/streaming, emphasizing efficiency and scalability across training/serving infrastructure, streaming/data pipelines, and storage systems.

Previously, I received my Master’s degree from CS@UMN, advised by Prof. Feng Qian.

Research

My research focuses on machine learning systems for multimodal/video and efficient model training & serving. Representative topics:

  • Resource efficient multimodal LLM inference (Zoomer, ViTL)
  • Learning based multimodal streaming systems (MLSys '25, NSDI '22, HotMobile '21)
  • Mobile immersive systems & networking (NSDI '24, ATC '21, MobiSys '20 Poster)
  • Disaggregated storage (SAN) architectures — (NSDI '26)
